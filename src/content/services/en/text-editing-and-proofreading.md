## What it is

Universitas text editing refines manuscripts, dissertations, theses, institutional reports, and technical briefings to ensure grammatical precision, logical clarity, and academic rigor.

We examine structural linguistic elements, including subject-verb agreement, syntax, sentence parallelism, punctuation, and smooth transitions between paragraphs, preserving the original voice of the author or team.

## Who it is for

- **Academia:** Researchers, faculty, and graduate students preparing dissertations, theses, peer-reviewed journal submissions, and grant proposals.
- **Business:** Analytical reports, white papers, commercial proposals, and corporate publications that demand polished prose.
- **Public Sector:** Policy assessments, terms of reference, executive summaries, and official publications.
- **NGOs and Civil Society:** Advocacy articles, annual accountability reports, and informative briefs for diverse stakeholders.

## How we work

1. **Diagnostic intake:** Review of the document type, target publication, audience, and requested editorial guidelines.
2. **Grammar and normative editing:** Detailed line-by-line correction of spelling, syntax, agreement, and punctuation.
3. **Stylistic refinement:** Improving rhythm, resolving ambiguous phrasing, and streamlining redundant terminology.
4. **Transparent delivery:** Providing tracked-changes files so authors retain full editorial authority over every adjustment.
