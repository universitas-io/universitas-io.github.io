## Overview

Universitas academic web design crafts elegant, accessible, and high-performance websites to showcase scientific contributions to peers, funding agencies, and the public. We build research laboratory portals, academic conference sites, and faculty portfolios.

We integrate scholarly identifiers (ORCID, Google Scholar, institutional repositories), inject specialized scientific indexing metadata, and maintain strict adherence to international accessibility standards (WCAG 2.2 AA).

## Who It's For

- **Academia:** Research laboratories (such as OEDLA and LABIIA), academic conference committees (such as the PPGCP-Unicamp Student Seminar), and university departments.
- **Businesses:** Corporate R&D divisions, think tanks, and technical consultancies publishing peer-reviewed reports.
- **Public Sector:** Scientific grant foundations and public research observatories.

## Our Methodology

We deliver across three architectural levels based on your team's maintenance preferences:

1. **Easy Visual CMS (WordPress):** For non-technical teams seeking intuitive visual page builders to update announcements and team rosters.
2. **Modern Static (Astro & Markdown):** Lightning-fast loading speeds, unhackable security, and Git-versioned Markdown content editing.
3. **Custom Full-Stack (Next.js & React):** Tailored web applications featuring integrated databases, submission forms, and dynamic data visualizers.
