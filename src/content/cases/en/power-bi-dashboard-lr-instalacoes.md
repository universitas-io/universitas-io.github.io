## Context

An engineering and technical facilities contractor operated with work orders, raw materials, and cost records fragmented across disparate spreadsheets maintained by field crews and administrative staff.

## The challenge

Executive leadership and project managers lacked consolidated, real-time oversight of construction progress, milestone billing, and operational bottlenecks. Monthly accounting reconciliations absorbed days of manual data aggregation.

## Methodological approach

Universitas designed a Business Intelligence workflow:

1. **Data cleaning and modeling:** Cleansing data feeds in Power Query, resolving redundant entries, and constructing a normalized star schema with dimension and fact tables.
2. **DAX measure engineering:** Formulating DAX calculations for contract profitability margins, milestone completion rates, and rolling cash flow projections.
3. **Interface design:** Clean executive interfaces with intuitive visual hierarchy, automated budget overrun alerts, and interactive cross-filtering by client, timeline, and technician crew.

## Results and impact

The unified Power BI dashboard reduced monthly reporting cycles from days to minutes, empowering leadership to track operational performance in real time and monitor project margins.
