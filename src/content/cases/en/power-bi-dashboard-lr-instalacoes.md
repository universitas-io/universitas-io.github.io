## Corporate Context

An engineering and technical facilities contractor operated with work orders, raw materials, and cost records fragmented across disparate spreadsheets maintained by field crews and administrative staff.

## The Challenge

Executive leadership and project managers lacked consolidated, real-time oversight of construction progress, milestone billing, and operational bottlenecks. Monthly accounting reconciliations absorbed days of manual data aggregation, with substantial risk of human data entry errors.

## Methodological Approach

Universitas designed a comprehensive Business Intelligence architecture:

1. **Data Cleaning & Modeling:** Cleansing data feeds in Power Query, resolving redundant entries, and constructing a normalized star schema with explicit dimension and fact tables.
2. **DAX Measure Engineering:** Writing robust DAX calculations for contract profitability margins, milestone completion rates, and rolling cash flow projections.
3. **User-Centric Visual Design:** Crafting clean executive interfaces with intuitive visual hierarchy, automated budget overrun alerts, and interactive cross-filtering by client, timeline, and technician crew.

## Results & Impact

The unified Power BI dashboard reduced monthly reporting cycles from days to minutes, empowering leadership to track operational performance in real time and preserve profit margins through proactive cost monitoring.
