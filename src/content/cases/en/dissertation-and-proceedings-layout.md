## Editorial Context

Publishing academic volumes, conference proceedings, and doctoral monographs requires meticulous adherence to institutional guidelines and editorial standards, coupled with professional typography that enhances readability and scholarly prestige.

## The Challenge

Extensive conference proceedings gather dozens of submissions from distinct authors with disparate citation conventions, unlinked footnotes, and formatting inconsistencies. Manually standardizing hundreds of pages is arduous and prone to formatting regressions.

## Methodological Approach

1. **Automated Citation Management:** End-to-end normalization of bibliographic entries utilizing Zotero and BibTeX engines, removing duplicate records and enforcing citation rules (ABNT, APA, Chicago).
2. **Professional Desktop Publishing:** Structuring typography in LaTeX and Adobe InDesign with regulated leading, margin geometry, and heading hierarchies.
3. **Figure and Table Optimization:** Treatment and vector conversion of complex charts and tables to ensure crisp rendering within strict print margin boundaries.

## Results & Impact

We delivered complete typesetting and editorial formatting for the **Master's Dissertation** and the **Electronic Proceedings of the XXXIV History Week at UFJF**, both published and indexed on Academia.edu. Both publications met strict submission deadlines and conformed fully with legal deposit criteria.
