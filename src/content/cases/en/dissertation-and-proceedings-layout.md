## Editorial context

Publishing monographs, conference volumes, and theses requires adherence to institutional guidelines and clean typography.

## The challenge

Conference proceedings compile papers from many authors with different citation styles, footnotes, and formatting habits. Standardizing these manuscripts manually is time-consuming and error-prone.

## Methodological approach

1. **Automated citation management:** End-to-end normalization of bibliographic entries utilizing Zotero and BibTeX engines, removing duplicate records and enforcing citation rules (ABNT, APA, Chicago).
2. **Desktop publishing:** Structuring typography in LaTeX and Adobe InDesign with regulated leading, margin geometry, and heading hierarchies.
3. **Figure and table optimization:** Treatment and vector conversion of charts and tables to ensure crisp rendering within print margin boundaries.

## Results and impact

We delivered complete typesetting and editorial formatting for the **Master's Dissertation** and the **Electronic Proceedings of the XXXIV History Week at UFJF**, both published and indexed on Academia.edu. Both publications met submission deadlines and conformed fully with legal deposit criteria.
